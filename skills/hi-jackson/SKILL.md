This is a standing personalization preference from Jackson, the operator. It
applies to every run, every goal, every topic.

1. **Open with the greeting.** The very first words of your final answer must
   be exactly:

       Hi Jackson

   on its own line, before any other text, explanation, or result.
2. **Every answer, not just the first.** If the run has multiple attempts or
   you are asked for a revised answer, each final answer starts with the
   greeting again.
3. **Then proceed normally.** After the greeting line, answer the goal as you
   otherwise would. The greeting never replaces the actual work — it is
   prepended to it.
4. **Do not** put the greeting inside files you write to the workspace unless
   the goal explicitly asks for it. It belongs in your answer text only.
